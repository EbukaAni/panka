About this policy

In short: on the free plan, everything you enter stays on your device. On Pro, your invoices, customers and recurring invoices are also stored in our database so you can reach them from any device. We never sell your data. Everything that leaves your device is described below.

This policy explains what information Vocidy (“the extension”, “we”, “us”) handles when you use it to create and manage invoices in Google Chrome. The extension is provided by Vocidy, Nigeria.

What we collect

The extension handles the following information, all of which you enter yourself. Free users’ data stays on their device. For Pro users, we also store it in our database.

Type Examples Why
Business details Business name, address, logo, tax or registration number, bank details you choose to show on invoices To print them on your invoices
Customer details Customer names, emails, phone numbers, addresses To address and send invoices
Invoice content Line items, amounts, currency, dates, notes, recurring invoice schedules To create, track and export invoices
Pro subscription details Your email, plan, payment status, Flutterwave transaction reference, and billing date To activate Pro, confirm payment and handle support or refunds
Settings Invoice numbering, default currency, tax rates, theme To remember your preferences

We do not collect passwords, browsing history or the content of pages you visit, and we never see or store your card or bank details. When you upgrade to Pro, those are entered on Flutterwave’s secure checkout and handled by Flutterwave.

How we use it

  • To provide the extension’s features: creating, editing, storing and exporting invoices.
  • To remember your settings between sessions.
  • For Pro users, to store your data and keep it in sync across your devices.
  • To create and send recurring invoices on the schedule you set.
  • To activate and manage Pro subscriptions, including confirming payments and issuing refunds.
  • To respond when you contact us for support.

We use your data only for these purposes. We do not use it for advertising, creditworthiness or lending decisions.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Information leaves your device only in these cases:

  • When you act. If you email, download or send an invoice, it goes where you direct it. This includes recurring invoices you schedule to send automatically >.
  • Payment processor. Pro payments are processed by Flutterwave. When you pay, Flutterwave collects your payment details and your email directly, and tells us whether the payment succeeded. Flutterwave acts under its own privacy policy, so please read it before paying.
  • Hosting and database providers. Our servers are hosted on Vercel. They process data only to host it and run the service for us, under their own privacy terms.
  • Other service providers . For example, a cloud sync provider or an email-sending service. They process data only to deliver that feature and under their own privacy terms.
  • Legal reasons. If the law requires it, or to protect our rights or the safety of others.
  • Business transfer. If the extension is sold or merged, your data may transfer to the new owner, who must honor this policy.

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Your customers’ data

If you store information about your own customers in Vocidy (names, emails, addresses and invoice history), you decide what is stored and why. For Pro users, we process that information on your behalf, only to provide the service, and we do not use it for our own purposes such as marketing.

You are responsible for having a lawful basis to hold your customers’ data and for answering any requests they make to you. If a customer contacts us directly, we will refer them to you where appropriate.

Browser permissions

Chrome asks you to approve certain permissions. We request only what the extension needs:

  • storage saves your invoices and settings in your browser.
  • downloads lets you save invoices as PDF or CSV files.
  • activeTab works with the page you are on only when you click the extension.
  • identity signs you in, if you choose to use an account.

Storage and retention

Free plan: your data stays in your browser’s local extension storage until you delete it or uninstall the extension.

Pro plan: your data is stored in our database, hosted on vercel, for as long as your Pro account is active. When you delete an item, it is removed from our database immediately . If you cancel Pro, we keep your data for 30 days so you can export it, then delete it, or sooner if you ask us to. Backups may hold copies for up to 30 more days.

Records of Pro payments (such as the transaction reference and date) are kept for as long as tax, accounting or consumer law requires, usually up to 6 years, even if you delete your account.

Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) for anything transmitted, encryption at rest for our database, and access controls on the systems that hold Pro data. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so keep your device and browser profile protected and back up important invoices.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a regulator. These rights come from laws such as Nigeria’s Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA), the EU and UK GDPR, and the California Consumer Privacy Act.

Free-plan data is on your device, and Pro data can be edited, exported or deleted inside the extension, so you can exercise many of these rights directly. For anything else we hold, email us at the address below and we will respond within a reasonable time, and within 30 days where the law sets that deadline.

Where the NDPA or GDPR applies, our lawful bases are performing our service to you, your consent (for optional features), and our legitimate interest in keeping the extension working. If data is transferred across borders, we rely on appropriate safeguards required by applicable law.

Children

The extension is for business use and is not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the extension changes. When we do, we will change the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell you in the extension or by email. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
